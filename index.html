😎About Me (冯云浩)

I am a Ph.D. student at the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), advised by Prof. Yanming Guo (郭延明). I was a visiting student at Fudan University from May to December 2025. I am currently an algorithm engineer intern at Ant Group, working on LLM and agent safety.

My long-term goal is to build safe, reliable, and controllable AI agents for real-world environments. My current research focuses on:

  • LLM and agent safety, backdoor attacks, and automated red teaming;
  • Safety post-training, guard models, and reinforcement learning;
  • Reliability evaluation of computer-use, coding, and research agents.

I am actively looking for academic collaboration on trustworthy LLMs and AI agents.
