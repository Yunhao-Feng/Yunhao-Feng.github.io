😎About Me (冯云浩)

I am a Ph.D. student at the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), advised by Prof. Yanming Guo (郭延明). I was a visiting student at Fudan University from May to December 2025. I am currently an algorithm engineer intern at Ant Group, working on LLM and agent safety.

My research aims to make AI agents safe, reliable, and controllable in real-world environments. I study how risks emerge across an agent’s reasoning and actions, and how evaluation, runtime safeguards, and post-training can improve its behavior. My interests include:

  • LLM & Agent Safety: backdoor attacks, prompt injection, and automated red teaming;
  • Safety Post-Training: guard models, reinforcement learning, and process supervision;
  • Trustworthy Agent Evaluation: computer-use agents, coding agents, and scholarly assistants.

I welcome research collaborations on trustworthy LLMs and AI agents. Feel free to get in touch!
